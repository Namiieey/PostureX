Posture habits

Build a comfortable routine
🧍‍♀️✨

Little resets, often

There is no single perfect posture. Vary your position, use comfortable support, and take breaks that work for you.

Everyday ideas

01

Screen-height check

Position your screen so you can look ahead without repeatedly bending your neck.

1 min
02

Comfortable movement break

Stand, walk, or gently change position if comfortable. Choose what feels right today.

2 min
03

Shoulder comfort check

Let your shoulders rest naturally; avoid forcing them down or holding a rigid pose.

30 sec
These are general wellness suggestions, not a prescribed treatment plan. A clinician can tailor advice to your symptoms and needs.

Back & neck care

Comfort-first education
🌿🧸

Listen to your body

Back and neck discomfort can have different causes. There is no one exercise that is right for everyone.

🪑

Adjust your environment

Try a comfortable chair, screen position and reachable work area.

🚶

Choose comfortable movement

Gentle everyday movement may be suitable for some people; do not push through pain.

🩺

Get individual guidance

A physiotherapist or clinician can assess persistent or recurring symptoms.

Seek urgent medical help for severe or rapidly worsening symptoms, new weakness, significant numbness, or symptoms following a serious injury.

Antenatal wellness

During pregnancy
🤰🌼

Support for changing needs

Pregnancy is individual. Discuss activity, pain, pelvic-floor care and any restrictions with your maternity team.

01

Comfort check-in

Notice what positions feel comfortable and take breaks when needed.

02

Everyday support

Use stable support when needed and avoid activities your clinician has advised against.

03

Ask your maternity team

Get personalised guidance before starting a new exercise or rehabilitation routine.

This section is educational, not a pregnancy exercise prescription. Stop activity and contact your maternity team for concerning symptoms; seek urgent care for emergencies.

Postnatal recovery

After birth
🫶🌷

Recovery has its own pace

Healing and readiness vary after birth. Your maternity team or pelvic-health physiotherapist can help you plan safe next steps.

01

Prioritise recovery

Rest when possible, accept support and follow your discharge instructions.

02

Ask about pelvic health

Discuss pelvic-floor symptoms, abdominal concerns, pain or leaking with a qualified clinician.

03

Return gradually

Ask when and how to resume exercise based on your birth, healing and symptoms.

This is general information, not a personalised postnatal rehabilitation plan. Seek prompt care for heavy bleeding, chest pain, trouble breathing, severe headache, fever, or other urgent symptoms.
